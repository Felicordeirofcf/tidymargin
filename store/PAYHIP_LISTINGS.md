- **File:** `TidyMargin-Starter.zip`
- **Description:**

Quote every cleaning job from YOUR real costs — not national averages.

- Turns square feet, bedrooms, bathrooms, service type, frequency and home condition into labor hours

- Gives you a price that hits your target profit MARGIN, rounded up, never below your minimum
- Shows the regular price before frequency discounts so recurring discounts don't eat your profit
