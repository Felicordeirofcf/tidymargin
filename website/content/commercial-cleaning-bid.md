# How to Bid a Commercial Cleaning Contract (Monthly Price Formula)

Commercial accounts are usually priced per month, not per visit. The method is the same as for homes - time, cost, margin - with a few differences.

## 1. Walk the space and agree the scope

Confirm the square footage by area type (open offices, private offices, restrooms, break rooms, lobbies), the tasks, the frequency, the time of day and who supplies paper goods and liners. Put the scope in writing.

## 2. Estimate hours per visit

Hours per visit = square feet / square feet per labor hour. Production rates depend heavily on the type of space, the layout and the tasks, so use your own timing whenever possible. If you have to start from an assumption, label it and check it after the first two weeks.

## 3. Convert to a month

Visits per month = visits per week x 52 / 12 (about 4.33 weeks per month).

## 4. Add up the monthly cost

- Labor = hours per visit x visits per month x pay x (1 + payroll burden).
- Supplies = supplies per visit x visits per month.
- Overhead assigned to the account: supervision, admin time, insurance share.

## 5. Price for your margin

> monthly price = monthly cost / (1 - target margin - card fee %)

Example with illustrative numbers: 5,000 sq ft at 2,500 sq ft per hour = 2 hours per visit, 3 visits a week = 13 visits a month. At $18/hour with 12% burden, $8 of supplies per visit and $150 of overhead, the monthly cost is $778.16. For a 30% margin with a 2.9% card fee, the monthly price is about $1,159.70, or about $89.21 per visit.

## 6. Review after 30 days

Compare the real hours with the bid. If the job takes longer, talk to the client early about the scope or the price.
